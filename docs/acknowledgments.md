## Workflow diagram tooling

The interactive BehaviorScope-X research workflow was generated with
[Archify](https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify), developed by
[`tt-a1i`](https://github.com/tt-a1i). Archify converts typed diagram sources
into validated, interactive HTML and SVG artifacts.

BehaviorScope-X keeps the authored diagram source in
[`behaviorscope-x.workflow.json`](behaviorscope-x.workflow.json) and publishes
the generated viewer as [`workflow-map.html`](workflow-map.html).

Archify is distributed under the
[MIT License](https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify/blob/main/LICENSE). We thank its
maintainers and contributors for making the workflow-rendering tools available.

The BehaviorScope-X workflow content, terminology, and site-specific styling
remain part of this repository. For the research manuscript, see the
[Citation](citation.md) page.
